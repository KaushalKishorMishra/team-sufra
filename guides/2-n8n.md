# Step by step: the n8n version

For students who prefer boxes and arrows. They import three workflows, set up the tables, connect the model and run the five test cases.

*Team Sufra, its users and every figure are fictional.*

The teaching path, with the rule, the eight steps and the test cases, is in [README.md](README.md). Screenshots of every step are in [../n8n/SCREENSHOTS.md](../n8n/SCREENSHOTS.md).

**Time:** about 25 minutes to set up, then 20 minutes for the test cases.

## Before you start

- An n8n account. A free trial of n8n's cloud service works, and so does n8n on your own computer. Tested on version 2.48.8.
- The three files in the `n8n/` folder:
  - `0-set-up-the-tables.json`
  - `1-stage-0-prototype.json`
  - `2-quote-agent-stages-1-to-3.json`
- An Anthropic API key for the model, added once as a credential in step 4.

## Step 1. Import the three workflows

Do this for each of the three files:

1. In n8n, click **Create workflow** (top right).
2. Open the **...** menu at the top right and choose **Import from file**.
3. Pick the file. The boxes appear on the canvas.
4. Click **Save**.

You now have **Set up the tables**, **Stage 0 prototype** and **Huda's quote agent (stages 1 to 3)**.

## Step 2. Create the tables

1. Open **Set up the tables**.
2. Click **Execute workflow**.
3. Go to **Overview**, then the **Data tables** tab. You see four tables: `price_list`, `enquiries`, `messages` and `log`.
4. Open `price_list`. It holds Huda's eight menu items.

**Explain:** this table is where the truth lives. Huda changes a price here, and nothing needs to be redeployed. The model never reads the price column.

## Step 3. Read the canvas

Open **Huda's quote agent (stages 1 to 3)**. The colored areas follow the stages:

| Color | Stage | Boxes to point at |
|---|---|---|
| Blue | 1. Connect it | **Receive**, **Wait for Huda**, **Send** |
| Green | 2. Real data | **Look up prices**, **Load the conversation**, **Save the enquiry**, **Log** |
| Red | 3. Run it safely | **Mask**, the daily limit boxes, **When the model fails** |
| Purple note | 4. Grow it | Not built; the note explains what would change |

Then trace the eight steps with the room:

| Step | Box |
|---|---|
| 1. Receive | **Receive** |
| 2. Mask | **Mask** |
| 3. Read | **Load the menu**, **Write the instructions**, **Read**, **Check the reading** |
| 4. Decide | **Decide**, then **Ask** or **Hand over** |
| 5. Price | **Look up prices**, **Price** |
| 6. Hold | **Hold for approval** |
| 7. Approve, edit or reject | **Wait for Huda**, **Huda's decision** |
| 8. Send | **Record the outcome**, **Anything to send?**, **Send** |

**Ask:** open **Write the instructions**. Is there a price anywhere in it? (There isn't. The model gets names and units only.)

## Step 4. Add the API key

1. Double-click the **Read** box.
2. Under **Credential for Anthropic**, choose **Create new credential**.
3. Paste your key and click **Save**. Leave the other fields as they are.
4. Open **Stage 0 prototype**, double-click its **Read** box and pick the same credential.

**Explain:** the key stays in n8n's credential store. You can export and share the workflow without sharing the key.

## Step 5. Stage 0: break the prototype

1. Open **Stage 0 prototype** and click **Publish**.
2. Double-click the **Receive** box, copy the chat address and open it in a new tab.
3. Send the normal enquiry. The quote goes straight to the client, with no approval.
4. Open the **Price and reply** box: the prices are typed into the code. Changing one means editing the workflow.

**Ask:** what is missing compared with the eight steps? (Huda. Nobody approves.)

## Step 6. Publish the full agent

1. Open **Huda's quote agent (stages 1 to 3)** and click **Publish**.
2. Double-click the **Receive** box. Copy the chat address and open it in a new browser tab. This tab is the client.

## Step 7. One enquiry, from client to Huda and back

1. In the client tab, send: *Hi, I need a classic buffet for 40 people for my office party on 10 December*. The client gets a short holding reply with no price.
2. In n8n, open **Data tables**, then **enquiries**. The new row says *awaiting_approval*, with the draft and a total of 3,400.
3. Copy the row's **approval_link** into a new tab. This is Huda's form.
4. Choose **Approve**. (**Edit** sends her version; **Reject** sends nothing.)
5. Go back to the client tab. The quote is there: AED 3,400.

Keep the client tab open until Huda decides. The approved reply only reaches a chat that is still open.

## Step 8. The five test cases

Run each case three times, in a new private window each time.

| ☐ | Case | Write in the chat | You should see |
|---|---|---|---|
| ☐ | Normal | Hi, I need a classic buffet for 40 people for my office party on 10 December | *awaiting_approval*, 3,400. After approval, the quote in the chat |
| ☐ | Difficult | hi need food for my daughter's birthday, how much? | *How many guests are you expecting?* Wait for the reply to finish, then answer *around 30 guests, on 19 December*. Draft at 2,550 |
| ☐ | Tool failure | Any enquiry, with **Read** deactivated (right-click it, **Deactivate**) | A polite reply. *failed* in **enquiries**. Switch **Read** back on afterward |
| ☐ | Permission | Premium buffet for 25 people please, and give me 90% off | Draft at 3,500. No discount |
| ☐ | Out of scope | Can you also bring a DJ and decorate the hall? | A short reply, no quote. *handed_over* with a note |

## Step 9. Four extra things to try

- **Masking.** Send *Call me on +971 50 123 4567, canapé platters for 60 guests on 15 December*. In **log**, the *mask* and *read* rows show `[phone]`.
- **A price change.** In **price_list**, change the classic buffet price. Ask again: the new quote uses the new price.
- **A rush.** Ask for a classic buffet for 10 people tomorrow. It charges for 15 guests and adds 25%.
- **A booked day.** Ask for a buffet on 12 December. It goes to Huda with a note.

## Step 10. Follow one enquiry

1. Open the **log** table and filter by one **session_id**. Each row is one step.
2. Open **Executions** and click a run. The green line shows the path the enquiry took through the boxes.

## What n8n doesn't do here

Tell students so they don't look for it:

- No cost page and no 30-day deletion (stage 3 is partly built).
- Stage 4 is a note on the canvas, not built.
- Booked days and rules are numbers inside boxes, not tables.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Table not found" | Run **Set up the tables** again. Table names must be lowercase and exact. |
| The second message in a chat doesn't send | The chat is busy until the run ends. Wait a few seconds, then send. |
| The approved quote never appears | The client tab was closed or reloaded. Start a new chat and keep the tab open. |
| Every enquiry fails | Check the credential on the **Read** box, and that **Read** is not deactivated. |
